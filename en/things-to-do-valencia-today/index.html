What's usually open on a Monday in Valencia

  • Mercado Central (Central Market): open 7:30 am – 3 pm (Monday to Saturday).
  • Heads-up: many museums close on Mondays (the Museo de Bellas Artes, Centre del Carme and L’ETNO open Tuesday to Sunday).
  • A good day for a walk in the Turia Gardens or on the beach — and for finding spontaneous plans nearby in the app.

Usual opening hours; they may change on public holidays or during events. Check before you go.

Things to do in Valencia today: FAQ

What to do in Valencia today?

It depends on the day and time: markets and museums in the morning, exhibitions, workshops and tardeo in the afternoon, and concerts, theatre or nightlife in Ruzafa and El Carmen at night. This page has a pick from the city agenda updated every day, and the WannaNow app shows on a live map everything happening near you.

What free things are there to do in Valencia today?

Some museums are free every day they open (such as the Museo de Bellas Artes, the Centre del Carme and L'ETNO), many municipal activities are free, and on Sundays and public holidays the municipal museums are free too. In WannaNow, the FREE filter shows only free plans near you.

How can I see what's happening right now in Valencia?

Open WannaNow and tap NOW: the map only shows events that are on and places that are open at that moment, near you. The app is in Spanish for now, but the map and filters are easy to use in any language.
